On the Killing Form

8/11/24

Killing Form

In a nutshell: the invariant symmetric bilinear form of the adjoint representation of a Lie Algebra.

Adjoint Representation

Given a Lie Algebra $ \mathfrak{g}$, one of the canonical representations of the Lie Algebra is the adjoint representation. This is obtained by the map on the elements of the lie algebra $\rho = x \mapsto [x, -]$.

Invariant Symmetric Bilinear Form (of a Lie algebra representation)

Given a representation of a Lie Algebra $V$ obtained by a map rho, the symmetric bilinear form is a bilinear map $B_V: V \times V \rightarrow k$, which takes the trace of the product of pairs of traces of elements in the representation.

$B_V(x,y) = \mathbf{tr}(\rho(x)\rho(y))$.

The Killing form is useful for classifying Lie algebras!
